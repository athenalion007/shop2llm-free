# Shop2LLM — AI Visibility & LLMs.txt Generator

[![Latest release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/athenalion007/shop2llm-free)](https://github.com/athenalion007/shop2llm-free/releases)

AI Search Visibility & GEO for WordPress — make your store discoverable by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity & AI search engines. AI visibility score, `llms.txt`, MCP server, AI crawler tracking, and AI SEO schema in 2 clicks.

Works with or without WooCommerce — product AI visibility for your store, plus blog/article AI visibility for your posts and pages.

**Install page:** https://shop2llm.com/install · **Docs:** https://shop2llm.com/fincore/v1/page/docs · **FAQ:** https://shop2llm.com/fincore/v1/page/faq

## Install in 3 steps

1. **Download** the latest [`shop-to-llm.zip`](../../releases/latest) (or from https://api.shop2llm.com/fincore/v1/download/free-plugin)
2. **Upload** in your WordPress admin: *Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin* → choose the zip → *Install Now*
3. **Activate** — llms.txt, JSON-LD product schema, the AI-readable catalog API, and AI crawler tracking go live immediately

Verify by visiting `https://yourstore.com/llms.txt`.

## Update channel

Release zips from this repository include a lightweight self-hosted update channel: your site checks `api.shop2llm.com` roughly twice a day, new versions appear under *Plugins → Updates*, and one-click automatic updates work like any wordpress.org plugin. You never need to re-download manually.

## Privacy

The GitHub-distribution build's update check sends only your **site URL** and **plugin / WordPress / PHP versions** to `api.shop2llm.com` — a per-site install census and compatibility check. No personal data, no store content, no analytics payloads.

The version distributed on [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/shop-to-llm/) contains **no such code**: wp.org installs never contact our servers and use the official wordpress.org update channel exclusively.
